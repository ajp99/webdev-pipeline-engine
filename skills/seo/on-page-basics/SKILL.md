# On page basics

## Preconditions

- Pages exist with front matter; `content/settings.json` holds the business name, address and contact details.

## Steps

1. Every page has a unique title and meta description from its front matter.
2. Open Graph and Twitter tags come from the same fields, with a share image from `public/`.
3. JSON-LD: `Organization`, or `LocalBusiness` (or the closest subtype the PRD names, such as `Dentist`, `LegalService` or `Restaurant`) generated from the settings, never typed by hand.
4. One `h1` per page; headings in order.
5. `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` are generated by the starter; check they list the built pages.
6. Images have width and height set and descriptive alt text.

## Decision guidance

- Do not add keyword stuffing or hidden text.
- Leave analytics and search-console verification out unless the PRD asks for them.
